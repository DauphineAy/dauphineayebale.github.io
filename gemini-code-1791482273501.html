Case Study 01 • Healthtech Product Lead Emory MPH Capstone • 2026

EndeleaCare: Low-Resource Closed-Loop Referral EHR

Architected a zero-licensing, offline-first Android referral EHR designed for rural regional catchments serving over 4 million individuals, eliminating unannounced emergency patient arrivals.

The Problem

100% of emergency transfers from primary health clinics arrived unannounced with lost paper records, creating severe triage bottlenecks and diagnostic lag.

The Architecture

Offline-first local SQLite caching on Android tablets paired with asynchronous delta-syncing via cellular handshake to an open-source HL7/FHIR store.

Quantified Impact

Targeted >60% loop closure rate (baseline 0%) and >80% record completion at arrival (n=87, α=0.05).

Case Study 02 • Knowledge Systems & MEL Living Water International • 2025 – Present

Global Knowledge Architecture & Frontline Telemetry

Consolidated enterprise information systems across 10 global country offices, cutting retrieval lag and scaling mobile field telemetry to over 10,000 active frontline workers.

The Problem

500+ field personnel across 10 country offices operated within fragmented reporting silos, causing delayed escalations and inefficient resource allocation.

The Solution

Built the 'Flourish' centralized SharePoint knowledge architecture; synthesized user interviews with 8 department leads into 12-network data SOPs.

Quantified Impact

Reduced info retrieval times by ~35%, steered $50K+ in emergency allocations via morbidity models, and boosted engagement ~30% via WCAG 2.1 overhaul.

Case Study 03 • Clinical Quality Improvement Kitante Medical Center • 2023 – 2025

Clinical Workflow Re-engineering & Digital Surveillance

Standardized digital intake protocols and automated statutory disease reporting triggers across pediatric, OB/GYN, and surgical inpatient/outpatient units.

The Problem

Clinical bottlenecks in neonatal and HIV cohorts produced diagnostic turnaround delays and sporadic compliance with national statutory health alerts.

The Solution

Standardized digital case intake tools across 3 wards and automated real-time alert triggers connecting the internal EMR to national reporting registries.

Quantified Impact

Reduced intake-to-diagnosis turnaround by 25% (2,500+ encounters), achieved 100% statutory alert compliance across 3,500+ records, and dropped diagnostic discrepancies 30%.
